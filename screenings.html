Showcasing independent films from emerging creators.

Ready to see some of the best films created by independent filmmakers? Here are some top films that will be streaming here, whether new or existing. All it matters that the films tells the story.

Top Films Screening Right Now

The Invite (2026)

In this feature-length comedy film featuring well-known actors such as Seth Rogan, Edward Norton, Penelope Cruz, and Olivia Wilde, who also directed this movie, tells of the enstranged couple Joe and Angela as their progressively frustration with each other reaches to a new level when Angela invite their free-spirited neighbors Pina and Hawk to dinner that slowly went out of control as the latter pair make a strange proposition to Joe and Angela. Featuring lots of sex-related comedy and surprising heartwearming and tearjerking moments, this film showcases what it means to be in a complicated relationship between a couple.

Fuelled (2021)

This 2D animated drama-thriller short film, directed by Michelle Hao and Emily Xu as a group project during the 3rd year at Sheridan College, tells a revenge story of the widow who, after having her home invaded and her husband murdered, seek revenge on the killer. When she ran out of gas during her crusade, she attempts to steal gas from the station, and once the attendant notices her, everything went to hell. With smooth animation, beautiful colors and compositions, and a warning message about the consequences of revenges, this short film will leave you at unease and eventually crying near the end of the film.

Flow (2024)

This critically-acclaimed feature-length film, created by Gints Zilbalodis, and widely known as the film made entirely with Blenders, tells the journey of a black cat as they attempt to survive the increasing flood with their fellow animals, with no humans present to help them. With colorful artstyle,, cute critters, a great plot premise and execution, and the adherence to the golden rule of silence, Flow is widely known as the masterpiece of the indie animation film industry.
