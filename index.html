Welcome

The Indie Film Collective is a creative community dedicated to independent filmmakers, storytellers, and visual artists.

Why This Community?

In the world of entertainment industry, visual-based creators, such as filmmakers, storytellers, and visual artists, are rather undervalued in the mainstream because their stories tends to be edited rather non-consensually under the studio executives' orders whenever the creators approves or not, such as sanitizing sensitive subjects like LGBTQ+ and limiting creative potentials for cinematography and the storyline. Unfortunately, when the executives are in charge of shaping the creator's works against their wills, then the creator's intentions won't be truly heard and conveyed, and thus won't inspire as much people as possible.

The Indie Film Collective solves this problem by inviting independent filmmakers to the community to show off their works in full display, with their intentions fully known to their heart's content. And in doing, their films might inspire people to take your beliefs and messages to their heart.

Collaborations

You can make a film all by yourself, whether the media type be in live-action or in animated format, but not only would that take at least a year to finish a film, but your creative storytelling, directing, accounting, and cinematography skills will be severely limited unless you are a master on one or two of them. To make your film shine and hasten the process a bit faster, you will need to collaborate with fellow creatives and even filmmakers to make your story become reality!
