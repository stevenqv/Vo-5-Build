Workshops

Hands-on learning in editing, cinematography, and storytelling.

Every person want to tell a story through a variety of mediums, from simple artwork to sculptures to even films. The latter is no exception, except that actually creating it is no easy task. You have to work together with your team to not only write a good story conveying only dialogues and scene directions, but also translate them to visual with cinematography skills. After filming the scenes, the team then must cut out and stitch the scenes together to create a coherent film base don their taste. Luckily, there are workshops stationed at Indie Film Collective that can help you improve those skills needed to create a great film.

Guides

Storytelling

The first step before filming is to plan out your ideas and convert them into a storyline. In this workshop, we will help students here learn about and improve on:

  • Brainstorming Ideas and Storyboards
  • The Basics of the Storyline
    • Characters and Dialogues
    • Structure of the Story
    • Settings
    • Tropes and Cliches
  • Format and the Fundamentals of Scripts
Listen to Expert's Tips Provided on Rain Dance's Article

Cinematography

The majority of the production consists of shooting the scene at a right angle, duration, and composition. In this workshop, we help students with:

  • What Cinematography Is and How They Work
  • How Cameras Work
  • Required Equipments and Setup
  • Shot Compositions and Framing
  • Types of Lighting Placements
Learn More on Studio Binder's Article on How to Master Cinematography Techniques

Post-Process Editing

After the team is done shooting the amount of scenes and are satisfied with its result, they would then edit those scenes so they would be combined together into a film with major editing in the process. In this workshop, we help students learn and improve on:

  • The Workflow of Editing Scenes
  • The Techniques of Scene Cuts
  • Applying Soundtrack and Sound Effects to Scenes
  • Apply visual elements like CGI (if needed)
  • Adjusting the Color Grading on Each Scene to Convey Moods and Emotions
  • Adding Subtitle Captions for International and Deaf Users
Learn More on BORISFX's Article Blog on What is Post-Processing Editing
