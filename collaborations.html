Collaborations

Connect with other creatives to bring ideas to life.

It's no easy creating to simply create a film from scratch, because you will need LOTS of teamworks from multiple people to not only make the film feel more high-quality, but allows for the project to be finished faster on time. In Indie Film Collective, we can help students who are struggling to find the right person capable for their jobs by suggesting which people is available to hire for the right job, such as actors based on their vocal range and performance.

If you don't know which person fits the right job for your film project, please see us at the work station in-person from 4:00 to 9:00 PM so our employees can discuss that topic you specified and give you suggestions and advices on hiring the right people for the job.
